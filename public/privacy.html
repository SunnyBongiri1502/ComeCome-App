Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Spark ek video matching application hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kya data collect karte hain aur usse kaise use karte hain.

Hum kya data lete hain

  • Naam aur age: jab aap "Find a Match" dabate hain, tab aapka diya hua naam aur age doosre matched user ko dikhaya jaata hai.
  • Camera aur Microphone: video call ke liye access chahiye hota hai. Ye access sirf tab use hota hai jab aap call par hote hain.

Hum data kaise store karte hain

Naam aur age sirf temporary memory me rakhe jaate hain jab tak aap app use kar rahe hain — koi permanent database ya account system nahi hai. App band karne ya disconnect hone par ye data hat jaata hai.

Video/audio call seedha do users ke devices ke beech connect hoti hai (peer-to-peer) — hum call ki recording nahi rakhte aur na hi usse access karte hain.

Third-party services

Ye app Render.com par hosted hai (hosting provider) aur Google ke STUN server ka use karti hai video call connect karne ke liye.

Age requirement

Ye app sirf 18 saal ya usse zyada age ke logon ke liye hai. Hum jaan-bujh kar minors se data collect nahi karte.

Aapke rights

Kyunki koi permanent account ya database nahi hai, aapka data automatically remove ho jaata hai jaise hi aap app se disconnect hote hain. Koi sawaal ho to neeche diye gaye contact par sampark karein.
